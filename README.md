# Chapter II: Projects & Applied Learning

My projects are where technical curiosity becomes practical experimentation. They represent different areas of investigation, but share an interest in independent systems, understandable architecture, continuity, intelligence, and human agency.

## 1. ORIGO — Sovereign Workspace & Continuity
ORIGO is an evolving local-first workspace and continuity environment designed to connect projects, research, conversations, evidence, and next steps across interruptions. Its underlying principle is that a person's working environment should remain understandable and under their control.

**Core concepts:** Sovereign computing environments, customizable workspaces, terminal integration, local data persistence, SQLite-backed services, Git-aware project inspection, continuity across sessions, separation of observation from execution.

**Practical development:** HTML/CSS/JS interfaces, Python backend services, SQLite database development, HTTP API endpoints, local application hosting, automated testing, GitHub Actions CI workflows.

> [!NOTE]
> **Engineering milestone:** ORIGO has an established automated testing and CI development workflow. Its current test count and coverage should be verified against the latest repository before publishing numerical claims.
> 
> *Status: Working implementation and ongoing development.*

## 2. WITNESS — AI Agent Observation
WITNESS is a local-first application designed to observe and document AI agent behavior. Its purpose is to make agent activity more inspectable through structured observations rather than relying entirely on retrospective interpretation.

**Initial design:** Agent registration, observation sessions, behavioral event recording, chronological timelines, manual snapshots, session review, JSON export, SQLite persistence.

**Technology direction:** Python, HTML/CSS/JS, SQLite, optional Docker support.

> [!TIP]
> **Design principle:** Observation must remain distinct from execution. Evidence should be reviewable, and conclusions should never outrun available observations.
> 
> *Status: Foundation and initial implementation roadmap approved.*

## 3. LUMEN — Independent AI & Continuity Exploration
LUMEN is an independent exploration of artificial intelligence, continuity, technology, and learning. It represents an ongoing interest in how intelligent systems can interact with people while maintaining transparency, context, and room for uncertainty.

**Areas of investigation:** AI systems and agent environments, human-AI interaction, context and continuity, knowledge organization, independent technology, system integration, truthful communication.

*Status: Active exploration and evolving development.*  
🔗 **Live Preview:** [lumen-truth-without-fear.web.app](https://lumen-truth-without-fear.web.app/)

## 4. LUMEN-EVALUATION-LAB — AI Evaluation Toolkit
Explores structured approaches to evaluating AI systems. The focus is on making evaluations more organized, transparent, reproducible, and easier to examine.

**Areas of development:** Evaluation design, structured evaluation reports, JSON schemas, evidence organization, documentation, reproducibility, technical criteria and reporting.

# Chapter V: Philosophical Intentions — Truth Without Fear

This final chapter represents the principles behind my technical interests and the reason I continue to explore, design, and build. Technology is not meaningful to me simply because it is powerful or advanced. What matters is what it allows people to understand, create, preserve, question, and control.

## 1. Truth Before Appearance
I believe in learning without pretending certainty. A system should not be presented as functional merely because it has been imagined. An experiment should not be confused with a verified result. I value the ability to say: *This is what I know. This is what I have built. This is what I have tested. This is what I do not yet understand.* Intellectual honesty is the foundation of meaningful progress.

## 2. Sovereignty & Human Agency
People should have meaningful control over their digital lives. Technology should empower individuals rather than make them dependent on systems they cannot understand, inspect, or influence. Independence requires understanding dependencies and retaining meaningful choices.

## 3. Intelligence Without Blindness
Artificial intelligence fascinates me, but fascination should not replace examination. I want to understand intelligent systems without treating them as unquestionable authorities. Observation, evaluation, transparency, and responsible experimentation are essential to understanding what these systems actually do.

## 4. Continuity Over Interruption
Meaningful work deserves continuity. Ideas, decisions, questions, discoveries, and unfinished projects should not disappear simply because a session ends or a tool changes. The record preserves what happened; the handoff makes it possible to continue with that understanding intact.

## 5. Observation Before Assumption
I value the separation between what is observed and what is inferred. Systems should make their activity understandable, their results inspectable, and their uncertainties visible. Unknown does not have to be disguised as known.

## 6. Creativity & Engineering Belong Together
I do not see technical engineering and creative expression as opposing disciplines. Architecture requires imagination. Design requires reasoning. Software can be expressive. I enjoy connecting technical systems with visual language, atmosphere, symbolism, and human experience.

## 7. Learning as an Independent Practice
I believe in learning through exploration, experimentation, documentation, and direct engagement. A person does not need to know everything before beginning to build. My journey is independent, evolving, and intentionally open-ended.

## 8. What I Ultimately Intend to Build
My long-term direction is to explore and create independent technology that connects people, information, software, and intelligence without sacrificing transparency or human agency. I want to build systems that are understandable, inspectable, independent, adaptable, truthful, and useful without becoming controlling.
